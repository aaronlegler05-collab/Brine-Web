FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need to write the scripts or come up with ideas?

No. Every package includes scripting. We learn your business and write hooks and scripts for you. You approve them before we film.

Where do you film?

We come to you, anywhere in Greeley, Evans, Windsor, Johnstown, Loveland, Fort Collins and the rest of Northern Colorado. Most shoots happen at your business, so the content looks like the place your customers will actually visit.

Is Meta ad spend included in the monthly price?

No. Package pricing covers our strategy, setup and management of your Meta ads. The ad budget itself is paid directly to Meta, and we'll recommend an amount that fits your goals before anything runs.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Monthly plans renew month to month. To cancel, email us before your next billing date. The full terms are in our Terms of Service.

What if I change my mind?

If you cancel before filming, you get a full refund. Once filming has happened, that work isn't refundable. Monthly plans can be cancelled before the next billing date; the current month isn't refunded. See the Refund Policy.

Can I start with one video and upgrade later?

Yes. The one-time shoot is a good way to see our work, and you can move to a monthly plan whenever you're ready.

Which platforms do you post to?

Our videos are made for Instagram Reels and Facebook, and they work on TikTok and YouTube Shorts too. We'll recommend the best mix for your customers.

What do you do with the information I send?

We use it only to reply to you and plan your project. The form below just emails your request to us (and a copy to you). Analytics and ad cookies only load if you accept them. You can ask us to delete your information at any time. See the Privacy Policy.

Ready to make your page stop the scroll?

Tell us a little about your business. We'll talk through your goals and which package makes sense. No pressure, and we reply within one business day.
