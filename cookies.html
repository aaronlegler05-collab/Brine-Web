The short version

This website sets no cookies. It has no analytics, no ad pixels, and no third-party trackers. The only thing it saves on your device is a note that you closed the cookie notice.

Cookies

Cookies are small files websites save in your browser. This site doesn't set any, and doesn't load anything from other companies that would.

Third parties

Our fonts are hosted on our own site, not loaded from Google or anyone else. Links to our Instagram and Facebook pages are ordinary links. Nothing from those sites loads here until you click one, and once you're on their sites, their own cookie policies apply.

Your controls

You can clear this site's stored data at any time in your browser's settings, usually under Privacy or Site data. The site works the same either way; the notice will just show again.

If this ever changes

If we ever add analytics or advertising tools, we'll update this policy first. Non-essential cookies will only load after you choose to accept them, and declining will be just as easy as accepting.
