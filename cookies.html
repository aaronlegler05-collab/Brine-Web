The short version

  • With your permission, we use Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel to see how people use this site and to measure our ads.
  • They only load after you click Accept. Declining is just as easy, and the site works the same either way.
  • If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a decline.
  • You can change your choice at any time.

Cookies

Cookies are small files websites save in your browser. Essential storage makes the site work. Analytics and advertising cookies are optional, and we only set them if you accept.

Third parties

If you accept, your browser connects to Google and Meta, and they receive information such as the pages you visit, your IP address, your device and browser, and whether you sent a booking request. We don't send them your name, email or anything you type into the form. Google and Meta handle that information under their own privacy policies: Google and Meta.

Our fonts are hosted on our own site. Links to our Instagram and Facebook pages are ordinary links, and nothing from those sites loads here until you click one.

Your controls

  • . If you switch from Accept to Decline, we stop loading these tools and remove their cookies from this site.
  • Turn on Global Privacy Control in your browser and we'll treat it as a decline automatically.
  • Clear cookies and site data in your browser settings at any time.
  • Opt out of Google Analytics on every site with Google's opt-out add-on, and manage Meta ads in your Facebook ad preferences.

Changes

If we add or change any tools, we'll update this policy and ask for your choice again.
