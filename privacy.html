The short version

  • We collect only what you choose to send us: your name, business, email, and optionally your phone and a message.
  • This website has no analytics, no ad pixels and no tracking cookies.
  • We don't sell your information or share it for advertising.
  • You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Who we are

Brine Creative is a creative marketing service based in Greeley, Colorado. In this policy, "we," "us" and "our" mean Brine Creative. This policy covers this website and the information you give us when you contact us or become a client.

What we collect

Information you send us

When you send the booking form, FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-delivery service, emails your request to us and emails you a copy. When you send the form, or email or call us directly, we receive:

  • your name and business name
  • your email address
  • your phone number, if you include it
  • which package you're interested in, and anything you write in your message
  • whether you asked to receive occasional updates by email

Information from client work

If you become a client, we also handle what's needed to do the work: scripts, footage and photos filmed at your business, details about your products and customers you choose to share, and access to the social media and Meta ad accounts you give us. People who appear on camera are filmed only with their knowledge.

What we don't collect

This website doesn't use analytics, advertising pixels, tracking cookies, or embedded third-party content. The booking form is delivered by FormSubmit. Our fonts are hosted on our own site. See the Cookie Policy.

Server logs

Like any website, our hosting provider may automatically keep basic technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested) to keep the site secure and working. We don't use these logs to identify or profile visitors.

How we use it

  • to reply to your inquiry and schedule a consult
  • to provide the services you hire us for, including posting content and running ads you approve
  • to send invoices and keep business records
  • to send occasional updates and offers, only if you opted in

We don't use your information for anything else without asking first.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for targeted advertising. We share only what's needed with:

  • Our email and file-storage providers, which hold our messages and project files.
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram), when we post content and run ad campaigns for clients. That work happens inside accounts the client controls or approves, under Meta's own terms and privacy policy.
  • FormSubmit, which delivers booking form requests to our inbox and sends you a copy.
  • Our payment processor, to take payments. We don't store card numbers.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

If you contact us but don't become a client, we delete your inquiry within 12 months. Client records are kept for as long as we work together and afterward for as long as tax and business record laws require. You can ask us to delete your information sooner (see Delete my data).

Children

Our services are for businesses, and this website is meant for adults. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18, and the booking form asks you to confirm you're 18 or older. If you believe a child has sent us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Emails and unsubscribing

We only send marketing emails if you opted in. Every marketing email we send includes our business name and mailing address and a working unsubscribe link. If you unsubscribe, we stop within 10 business days. We'll still reply to messages you send us and send emails about active client work.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we have about you, and give you a copy
  • correct information that's wrong
  • delete your information
  • stop sending you marketing emails

We honor these requests for everyone, wherever they live, and we won't treat you differently for making one. We respond within 45 days. We may need to confirm it's really you before acting, usually by replying from the email address we have on file.

Delete my data

To have your information deleted, email BrineCreative@brinemarketing.com with the subject "Data deletion request," from the email address you used with us. Tell us your name and business name. We'll confirm when it's done. We may keep records the law requires us to keep, such as invoices, and we'll tell you if that applies.

Security

We keep client files and account access limited to the people working on your project, and we use reputable providers with their own security protections. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so we also keep what we collect to a minimum.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If a change affects how we use information you've already given us, we'll ask first.
